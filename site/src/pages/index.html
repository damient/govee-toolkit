Control your Govee lights from your own machine.

Your lights stay on your network, and your commands stay there with them: over Wi-Fi, over Bluetooth, from a lighting desk in DMX, or through the cloud on the days you are away.

Paint the zones, not the whole light.

The strip below draws one zone per address on the wire. A pointer over it lights the zones it passes.

  • One zone at a time

    Address a single zone, a range or the whole strip, inside the same frame.

  • Build your own effects

    A wave, a gradient, a progress bar across the zones: you write the animation, and the strip draws it.

  • Frame after frame

    Hold the channel open and keep sending. Music, screen, a sensor: the strip follows.

Main features

Find your lights, drive them, and paint them zone by zone. Every one of these is one line in a terminal, or one line of code.

Scan

One command asks your network who is there. Every light in Wi-Fi or Bluetooth range answers with its name.

On, off, color, brightness

The everyday commands, from a script, a keyboard shortcut or your home automation. Anything that runs code drives the light.

Every segment, one by one

Your strip is a row of zones. Give each zone its own color and build the look the preset effects do not give you.

Animation in real time

Hold the channel open and send frame after frame. The light follows your music, your screen or any source you write.

One engine, written once.

Use the language you already write in. Rust, Python and Node.js all drive the lights the same way, because one engine does the work under each of them.
