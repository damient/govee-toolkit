Control your Govee lights from your own machine.

No internet, no Govee account, no cloud round-trip. The command goes straight from your computer to the light, over your Wi-Fi or over Bluetooth. It also sends commands the official app does not expose.

Take control of my lights Does my device work? 
$ cargo install govee-toolkit-cli
$ govee scan
rope        H61A0   lan   ok
car-strip   H6114   ble   ok
$ govee on rope
$ govee color rope "#ff3d00"
$ govee segment rope --zones 1,2,3 "#45e3d0"

Paint the zones, not the whole light.

Move over the strip below. Each zone is one address on the wire.

  • One zone at a time

    Address a single zone, a range or the whole strip, inside the same frame.

  • Build your own effects

    A wave, a gradient, a progress bar across the zones: you write the animation, and the strip draws it.

  • Frame after frame

    Hold the channel open and keep sending. Music, screen, a sensor: the strip follows.

Main features

On, off, brightness, color

One call, from a script, a keyboard shortcut or a home automation. Anything that can run code can drive the light.

Every segment, one by one

The app offers a set of preset effects. Here you address the LED zones yourself, and on the fast path you reach more zones than the app shows.

Animation in real time

Open the segment channel and feed it frames: music reactive, screen ambilight, or your own source.

A new device onto your Wi-Fi

Over Bluetooth, the toolkit hands a device out of the box your network name and password. That step is what makes it reachable over Wi-Fi afterwards.

One engine, written once.

The protocol lives in Rust. Python and Node.js bind to that same core, so every language sends the same bytes.

You choose the path.

lan

Fastest · same Wi-Fi

Everything, segments included. Turn on "LAN Control" in the Govee app first.

ble

Fast · Bluetooth range

Works with no Wi-Fi at all. It also puts a new device on your network.

cloud

Slowest · anywhere

Through Govee's own API, with a key. Throttled, and no animation.
