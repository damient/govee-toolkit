The small print, which is small

Which lights are worth sending?
Any one missing from this list. A strip, a bulb, a lamp, a panel: if it lights up and it is a Govee, it is welcome. A model on Govee's own list of Wi-Fi models is the easy kind, and it still needs somebody to confirm it.
Where does my light end up?
On our shelf, for good. It is your contribution to the project, and it earns its keep there: it answers questions long after its page goes up, every time the firmware changes. Take lane one if you would rather keep yours.
What if my model resists?
Then the page says so, in those words: untested, which is a long way from broken.
